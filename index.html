About me

Guillermo Sosa-Gómez holds a Licenciatura in Mathematics (2007) and a Master’s degree in Applied Mathematics (2010) from Universidad Central “Marta Abreu” de Las Villas (UCLV), Cuba, and a Doctorate in Sciences with a specialization in Basic Mathematics from the Centro de Investigación en Matemáticas (CIMAT), Mexico. Since 2019 he has been Professor-Researcher (Titular C) at Universidad Panamericana, Guadalajara campus, and he is a member of Mexico’s Sistema Nacional de Investigadoras e Investigadores (SNI/SECIHTI, Level I). Since 2025 he is an Affiliated Staff Member of HSE University (Saint Petersburg, Russia).

His research covers cryptography and information security, Boolean functions and S-boxes resistant to power attacks, randomness testing, combinatorial optimization and metaheuristics, operations research and logistics, applied machine learning, multifractal analysis, and the security of graphical authentication. His work has appeared in journals such as Annals of Operations Research, IEEE Access, Physica A, Theoretical Computer Science, Entropy, Frontiers in Artificial Intelligence and the Journal of Information Security and Applications. A parallel line applies Bayesian hierarchical models and structural equation modeling to higher-education rankings, sustainability and entrepreneurship. His optimization and machine-learning models for automatic lead scoring and sales-advisor assignment were transferred to an industrial company.

He has supervised bachelor’s, master’s and doctoral theses in Mexico and Cuba, leads the Panamerican Entrepreneurship Hub at UP, was Guest Editor of special issues of Axioms (2023–24) and Cryptography (2024–25), is Guest Editor of the Mathematics (MDPI) Special Issue “Boolean Functions in Cryptography”, and has refereed for international journals. He collaborates with institutions in Cuba, Russia, Brazil, Colombia, Indonesia and Malaysia.
